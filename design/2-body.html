Sci-Fi Character HUDInteractive prototype. Rolls here are simulated; in Foundry every button runs the game system's own code.

Small screens: press H to hide the whole HUD and again to bring it back (rebindable in the gear dialog). Chrome drops the device art and then the background entirely, leaving only the panels; Window simulates a smaller monitor.
Power: use the Power button, or the standby panel on a dormant device, to play the activation and shutdown sequence — the omni-tool and EVA screens rise out of the device, the wrist computer folds down. Switching theme replays it.
Try: the tab buttons, any roll (strikes use the multiple attack penalty), HP −/+, hero points, conditions, carry states in Inventory, and the Paragon/Renegade tracks in the Dossier. Hover or click anything with a name to see it in the readout panel. The gear in the name bar opens the per-character theme picker. Sample characters only: Asha uses real items from the Mass Effect conversion; Dana's and Vex's gear is placeholder.
